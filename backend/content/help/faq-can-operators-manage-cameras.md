![The Add Camera dialog — available to Operators, not just
Administrators](/help/add-camera-modal.png)

Yes. Adding, editing, enabling/disabling, and removing cameras is available to
Operators, not just Administrators — camera management is treated as a day-to-day
operational task, not an admin privilege, since it's Operators who are most often the
ones noticing a camera needs attention in the first place.

What's still Administrator-only is managing _user accounts_, the audit trail, and
backup/restore — see [Getting Started with ADAS](getting-started-with-adas) for the
full split between the two roles, and [Adding, Editing, and Disabling
Cameras](managing-cameras) for how camera management itself works.
