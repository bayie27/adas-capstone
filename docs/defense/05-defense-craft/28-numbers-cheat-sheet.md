## Discrepancies found

- **TensorRT export batch:** Guide 27 repeats the paper’s Table 23 profile of **15**, while Guide 25 says the paper’s export section lists **32**; the current Test Plan model configuration names dynamic maximum/optimum **10**, which matches the immediate target and current tracker profiles. Quote **10 for the adopted current configuration**; describe 15/32 as historical paper figures until the paper is corrected. Guides 25 and 27 need that context made explicit.
- **Participant count:** the tracker’s UAT Traceability `G43` says “eight participants,” but the Test Plan, Session Control, SUS sheet, and UAT Results record **four** participants (3 Operators, 1 Administrator) and **four** SUS responses. Quote four; correct the traceability note.
- **Stream target versus qualification:** Guide 27 mentions the paper’s **15** batch/profile point, while the Test Plan sets **10** as the immediate target and the tracker actually exercises **8** plus a **9** step. Quote each as its proper register: 10 target, 8 clean qualification, 9 degradation step, 15 deferred.
- **“100%” language:** the 194/194 and 33/33 figures are test case and participant stage pass rates. They must not be repeated as model recall; the operational event result remains **8/16**, including **0/6** hard clips.
