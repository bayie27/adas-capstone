title: Who can see the audit trail?
category: FAQ
roles: [Admin, Operator]
summary: Administrators only — Operators don't have access to it.
sort_order: 70
is_faq: true
---

Only Administrators can view the audit trail. Operators don't have access to it, even
though many of the entries in it are their own actions.
