title: What happens if I don't respond to an alert?
category: FAQ
roles: [Admin, Operator]
summary: Nothing times out on its own — the popup and the alarm stay active until someone acts.
sort_order: 16
is_faq: true
---

The system never auto-confirms or auto-dismisses an alert for you. The alert popup and
its alarm keep going indefinitely until a person confirms it, dismisses it, or — on a
shared workstation — a colleague acts on it first. If the sound is more than you can
work with for a moment, you can snooze it temporarily without losing the alert; see [The
Accident Alert Popup and the Alarm](the-accident-alert-popup-and-the-alarm).
