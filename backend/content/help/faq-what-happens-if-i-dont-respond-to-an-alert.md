![The full-screen accident alert: it stays up until someone confirms or dismisses
it](/help/accident-alert-popup.png)

The system never auto-confirms or auto-dismisses an alert for you. The alert popup and
its alarm keep going indefinitely until a person confirms it, dismisses it, or, on a
shared workstation, a colleague acts on it first. If the sound is more than you can
work with for a moment, you can snooze it temporarily without losing the alert; see
[The Accident Alert Popup and the Alarm](the-accident-alert-popup-and-the-alarm).

There's no penalty for taking a little time to look closely before deciding, but be
aware that leaving an alert unanswered means the camera that raised it stays paused the
whole time. See [Camera Connection Status vs. AI Status](camera-connection-vs-ai-status),
so an alert sitting unanswered for a long stretch also means that location isn't being
actively monitored for a new event during that stretch.
