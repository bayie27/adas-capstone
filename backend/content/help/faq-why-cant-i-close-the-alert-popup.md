title: Why can't I close the accident alert popup?
category: FAQ
roles: [Admin, Operator]
summary: There's no close button or Escape shortcut on purpose — you have to confirm or dismiss it.
sort_order: 15
is_faq: true
---

It's intentional. A real accident should never be able to disappear from view because of
a stray click or an accidental Escape key press. The only ways to clear the popup are to
confirm it as a real accident or dismiss it as a false positive. If you need a moment
before deciding, use the snooze button to pause the alarm sound without closing the
popup — see [The Accident Alert Popup and the Alarm](the-accident-alert-popup-and-the-alarm).
