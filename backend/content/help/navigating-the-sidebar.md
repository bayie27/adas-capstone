## Why the sidebar looks different depending on who's logged in

Every section listed here exists in the app, but not every section appears for every
account. The sidebar only ever shows what your role is allowed to open — if something
below is missing from your own view, that's expected, not a bug. See [Operator vs.
Administrator: What Each Role Can Do](operator-vs-administrator-roles) for the full
breakdown.

## Sections available to everyone

- **Dashboard** — the daily overview: key numbers and recent activity at a glance. See
  [Reading the Dashboard KPIs](reading-the-dashboard-kpis).
- **Cameras** — the list of registered cameras and their live status. See [Camera
  Connection Status vs. AI Status](camera-connection-vs-ai-status).
- **Detections** — the full history of every alert the system has ever raised, with
  search and filters. See [Viewing and Filtering Incidents](viewing-and-filtering-incidents).
- **System Health** — server and hardware diagnostics: is the AI engine running, is the
  database reachable, and so on. See [Monitoring System Health](monitoring-system-health).
- **Help Center** — this page. Searchable, and organized the same way for every role,
  minus whatever sections your role can't open.
- **Profile** — your own name, username, password, and alarm sound preferences. See
  [Updating Your Profile and Password](updating-your-profile-and-password).

## Sections only Administrators see

- **AI Performance** — detection-model accuracy, precision, and per-camera performance
  over time.
- **Users** — creating accounts and assigning roles.
- **Audit Log** — a record of every recorded action taken in the system, by whom. See
  [Who Can See the Audit Trail?](faq-who-can-see-the-audit-trail) for who has access.
- **Maintenance** — backing up and restoring the system's database.

These four have their own Administrator-only Help Center guides. If you're signed in as
an Operator, those guides won't open — that's the role boundary working as intended,
not a broken link.

## Where the ONGOING tray fits in

Separately from the sidebar, a floating tray listing every currently open incident
follows you around the app no matter which page you're on. It isn't a sidebar section
because it's always available, not something you navigate to. See [Reviewing Ongoing
Incidents in the Tray](reviewing-ongoing-incidents-in-the-tray).

## If you can't find something

Use the search bar at the top of this Help Center rather than guessing which sidebar
section it belongs to — searching by what you're trying to do usually gets you there
faster than browsing by category.
