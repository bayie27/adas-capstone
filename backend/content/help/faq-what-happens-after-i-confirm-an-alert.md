title: What happens after I confirm an alert?
category: FAQ
roles: [Admin, Operator]
summary: It moves to Ongoing and the camera that reported it stays paused until you resolve or correct it.
sort_order: 10
is_faq: true
---

It moves to **Ongoing**, you're recorded as the verifying operator, and the camera that
reported it stays paused. It stays that way until you either resolve it (scene cleared)
or dismiss it as a correction (it wasn't a real accident after all).
