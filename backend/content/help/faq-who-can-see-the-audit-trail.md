![The Audit Log — visible to Administrator accounts only](/help/audit-log.png)

Only Administrators can view the audit trail. Operators don't have access to it, even
though many of the entries in it are their own actions — logging in, confirming or
dismissing an alert, or editing a camera all still get recorded either way, whether or
not the person who did them can later look the record up themselves.

See [Reading the Audit Trail](reading-the-audit-trail) for what's actually recorded and
how to search it, and [Getting Started with ADAS](getting-started-with-adas) for the
full list of what's Administrator-only versus available to everyone.
